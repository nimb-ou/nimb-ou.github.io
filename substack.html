1. Foundational Theory & Mathematics

Email open rates and newsletter retention are governed by cognitive load bounds and information-theoretic hook surprise.

Flesch-Kincaid Readability Formulation

To quantify whether prose is accessible or needlessly convoluted, we compute the Flesch Reading Ease (FRE) and Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level (FKGL):

\text{FRE} = 206.835 - 1.015 \left(\frac{\text{words}}{\text{sentences}}\right) - 84.6 \left(\frac{\text{syllables}}{\text{words}}\right)
\text{FKGL} = 0.39 \left(\frac{\text{words}}{\text{sentences}}\right) + 11.8 \left(\frac{\text{syllables}}{\text{words}}\right) - 15.59

Top-performing technical newsletters typically score in the 60–70 range (Grade 8–10): technical concepts explained with concise sentence structures and active verbs.

Predictive Click-Through Rate Logistic Model

The headline score \(\hat{y} \in [0, 100]\) maps five orthogonal signals through a calibrated logistic function:

\hat{y} = 100 \cdot \sigma\Big( \beta_0 + \beta_1 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{\text{has\_number}} + \beta_2 \cdot \text{PowerWordRatio} - \beta_3 \cdot \max(0, \text{Len} - 65) - \beta_4 \cdot \text{PassiveVoicePenalty} \Big)

Mobile email clients (Gmail, Apple Mail) truncate subject lines beyond 65 characters; hence, exceeding this threshold incurs an exponential penalty.

4. Failures, Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

Service Worker Dormancy & State Loss

Under Manifest V3, background workers terminate after 30 seconds of inactivity to save battery. In early builds, author session statistics stored in memory variables were lost mid-typing. Fix: All state migrated to asynchronous chrome.storage.local, enabling instant seamless resumption without interrupting the writer.

ProseMirror Keystroke DOM Flooding

The Substack rich-text editor (built on ProseMirror) fires multiple DOM mutations per keystroke. Running tokenization on every event caused fan spin and UI lag. Implementing a 250ms trailing debounce reduced DOM extractions by 94% while maintaining the perception of instant real-time feedback.
