Product Philosophy. Modern content creation is bogged down by fragmentation: writers research keywords in one tab, draft in another, and inspect subscriber funnels in a third. This agent collapses the entire loop into a contextual sidebar inside Substack, providing real-time critique on pacing, hook strength, and title click-through potential.

1. System Architecture

1. Ambient Chrome Extension

Lightweight content script that detects active draft editor states, parses live markdown structures, and communicates with background service workers via secure message ports.

2. SEO & Trend Engine

Scans high-growth newsletter verticals, extracts high-performing question headers, and suggests counter-intuitive hooks calibrated for viral Substack Notes distribution.

3. Multi-Tier LLM Orchestrator

Routes micro-edits and grammar polish to fast sub-second models while reserving high-reasoning frontier models for narrative structure analysis and paywall cutoff placement.
