Splitwise Debt Settlement Engine

An expense sharing backend and optimization engine. Formulates multi-party group debts as directed cash-flow graphs and implements greedy minimum cash-flow bipartite matching to eliminate circular balances, condensing dozens of interpersonal debts into the minimum possible transactions.

Algorithmic Complexity. In a group of \(N\) members with pairwise transactions, total debts scale as \(O(N^2)\). By computing net member credit/debit balances and iteratively pairing the largest debtor with the largest creditor, the number of settlement transactions is bounded strictly by \(N - 1\).
