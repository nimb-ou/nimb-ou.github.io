1. Foundational Theory & Mathematics

Natural color imagery lies on low-dimensional non-linear sub-manifolds embedded within astronomical Euclidean spaces.

The Curse of Dimensionality in \(\mathbb{R}^{3072}\)

A \(32 \times 32 \times 3\) color image contains \(D = 3,072\) continuous color features. In high-dimensional spaces, the ratio between the distance to the nearest neighbor and the distance to the farthest neighbor approaches 1:

$$\lim_{D \to \infty} \frac{\text{dist}_{\max} - \text{dist}_{\min}}{\text{dist}_{\min}} \to 0$$

Direct Euclidean distance over raw pixels fails because random background noise dominates foreground semantic differences.

Principal Component Analysis & Covariance Diagonalization

To isolate true structural variations (edges, silhouettes, chromatic profiles) from pixel noise, we compute the sample covariance matrix \(\mathbf{\Sigma}\) across \(N = 50,000\) training examples:

$$\mathbf{\Sigma} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N (\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{\mu})(\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{\mu})^T = \mathbf{V} \mathbf{\Lambda} \mathbf{V}^T$$

Selecting the top \(k = 50\) eigenvectors \(\mathbf{V}_k \in \mathbb{R}^{3072 \times 50}\) captures 83.4% of total dataset variance while compressing the storage footprint by 98.4%. Each class prototype \(\mathbf{p}_c\) is synthesized as the class conditional centroid:

$$\mathbf{p}_c = \frac{1}{|S_c|} \sum_{i \in S_c} \mathbf{V}_k^T (\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{\mu}) \in \mathbb{R}^{50}$$

4. Failures, Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

Background Chromatic Dominance

In early uncentered tests, airplanes and ships frequently confused because both featured dominant blue backgrounds (sky and ocean). Whitening the covariance matrix (\(\mathbf{\Sigma}^{-1/2}\)) forced the eigenspaces to focus on vehicle silhouettes rather than background color.

Biological vs Engineering Ceilings

While 39.08% accuracy sounds low compared to modern ResNets (95%+), it represents an instructive thermodynamic baseline: it is the exact upper bound achievable by pure spatial linear projection in under 3 milliseconds on CPU without iterative gradient adjustments.
