Core Design Thesis. Debt collection is fundamentally a contact-timing and liquidity-alignment problem, not a volume problem. Flooding delinquent accounts with repetitive calls triggers churn and legal liability. Collections OS models debtor cashflow rhythms and dispatches timely settlement options via WhatsApp, SMS, and email under strict regulatory contact limits.

1. Architecture & Data Contracts

Built around modern data stack best practices: raw ingestion events adhere to schema-enforced data contracts before landing in warehouse staging tables.

1. Ingestion & Synthetic Gen

Parameterized generator creating realistic longitudinal payment histories, bank SMS signals, and bounce events with realistic seasonality and macroeconomic shock injections.

2. dbt Staging & Marts

Modular transformations creating clean dimensional marts: dim_borrowers, fct_payment_attempts, and fct_dunning_events with automated freshness and uniqueness assertions.

3. Strategy & Compliance Engine

Dynamic policy router enforcing cooldown windows, time-of-day contact rules (FDCPA & RBI compliance), and dispute flags before emitting communication payloads.

2. Bounce Prediction & Action Triggers

Behavioral Bounce Model

Features: Rolling 30/60/90-day debit attempt failure rate, salary credit date alignment, average settlement discount requested, and communication responsiveness.

Loss Formulation: Asymmetric cost matrix penalizing late follow-up on high-balance accounts while avoiding unnecessary harassment of self-curing accounts.

Omnichannel Delivery Dispatch

Dynamic Cadence: Adjusts messaging tone from friendly reminder to structured EMI restructuring proposal based on debtor liquidity tier.

Automated Fallback: Webhook listeners track delivery, open, and click-through rates; failed channels escalate gracefully without duplicate notifications.

3. Run & Deploy

git clone https://github.com/nimb-ou/collections-os
cd collections-os
make venv
docker-compose up -d     # Starts Postgres, Dagster webserver, and daemon
make seed-data           # Generates synthetic borrower data
dagster dev              # http://localhost:3000
