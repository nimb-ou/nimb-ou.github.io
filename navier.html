The Clay Millennium Problem Context. The Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem asks whether smooth, physically reasonable solutions exist globally for all time in three dimensions. This project combines formal verification of known analytical bounds (Sobolev embedding, Leray-Hopf weak solutions, Kato-Ponce inequalities) with computational searches for potential singularity blowups.

1. Lean 4 Formalization Highlights

Euler.lean

Formal definition of the inviscid Euler momentum balance on torus \(\mathbb{T}^3\), divergence-free vector field constraints, and complete machine-verified proof of kinetic energy conservation for smooth solutions.

NavierStokes.lean

Viscous dissipative operator formulation with positive kinematic viscosity \(\nu > 0\), pressure projection via Leray projector, and energy inequality formalization bounded by initial kinetic energy.

Mathlib Integration

Leverages Lean 4 calculus, Sobolev spaces \(H^s(\mathbb{R}^3)\), and distribution theory to formalize weak solutions without unproven axioms.

2. Numerical Simulation & Visualizer

Singularity Simulation Suite

Initial Conditions: Perturbed Taylor-Green vortex and anti-parallel vortex tube configurations designed to maximize enstrophy growth.

Adaptive Timestepping: Runge-Kutta 4th order with dynamic CFL condition tracking rapid gradient steepening up to \(t = 0.90\).

Web-Based Interactive Explainer

Built-in Web Lab: Self-contained interactive simulator in docs/index.html with KaTeX mathematical derivations and real-time slice plots.

Static Plot Gallery: Rendered vorticity isosurfaces and cross-sectional velocity profiles demonstrating boundary layer dissipation.
