1. Foundational Theory & Mathematics

Detecting team friction requires separating healthy natural variance from genuine structural alienation.

Isolation Forest Path Length Formulation

Anomalies are few and different, making them susceptible to isolation early in random recursive partition trees \(iTrees\). Given a feature vector \(\mathbf{x}\), the anomaly score \(s(\mathbf{x}, n)\) over \(n\) samples is:

s(\mathbf{x}, n) = 2^{-\frac{\mathbb{E}(h(\mathbf{x}))}{c(n)}}, \quad \text{where } c(n) = 2\ln(n - 1) + 0.5772156649 - \frac{2(n - 1)}{n}

- If \(\mathbb{E}(h(\mathbf{x})) \to 0 \implies s \to 1\): \(\mathbf{x}\) isolates quickly (high anomaly / burnout candidate).
- If \(\mathbb{E}(h(\mathbf{x})) \to n - 1 \implies s \to 0\): \(\mathbf{x}\) requires deep partitioning (normal team member).

k-Means Behavioral Archetype Clustering

Reviews are embedded across four dimensions: normalized score, collaboration percentage, withdrawn descriptor ratio, and 7-day velocity. Minimizing inertia yields 4 distinct organizational archetypes:

\arg\min_{\mathbf{S}} \sum_{i=1}^4 \sum_{\mathbf{x} \in S_i} \|\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu}_i\|^2 \implies \{\text{Catalyst}, \text{Anchor}, \text{Solo Specialist}, \text{At-Risk}\}

4. Failures, Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

The Static Score Trap

Traditional HR monitors flag employees whose scores drop below 3.0/5.0. However, high performers burning out often maintain high quality (3.8/5.0) while their peer collaboration drops sharply. Isolation Forest caught these patterns 21 days before any drop in absolute quality.

Privacy & PII Protection

Raw text comments contain sensitive personal details. The model operates exclusively on tokenized behavioral tags and aggregated numerical features, ensuring employee psychological safety.
