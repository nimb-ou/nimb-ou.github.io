Power Engineering Context. In electric vehicle drivetrains and industrial motor controllers, inverter switching losses dominate thermal limits. Discontinuous PWM clamps each phase leg to the positive or negative DC rail for 60° of the fundamental cycle, completely eliminating switching events during peak current intervals.

1. Methodologies Compared

Sine-Triangle (SPWM)

Classical carrier-based modulation with third-harmonic injection to extend linear modulation index up to \(2/\sqrt{3} \approx 1.15\).

Space Vector (SVPWM)

Seven-segment switching sequence in the \(\alpha\beta\) stationary reference frame, centering zero vectors to minimize total harmonic ripple.

Discontinuous (DPWM1 & DPWMMAX)

Clamping modulation strategies reducing high-frequency switching transitions by one third without compromising fundamental voltage output.
