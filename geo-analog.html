The Analog Search Problem. Traditional real estate and commercial planning evaluates locations through crude zip-code medians. GeoAnalog constructs dense feature embeddings from high-resolution spatial points-of-interest (within 1km radius), matching urban vibes across continents (e.g. finding that Williamsburg Brooklyn directly matches Le Marais in Paris and Belleville).

1. Four Core Capabilities

1. Neighborhood Vibe & Profile

Evaluates any global address with A–F scores across 5 urban dimensions (walkability, transit access, community spaces, lifestyle density, and convenience) alongside matched global twins.

2. Relocation Decision Engine

Head-to-head comparison for 2–4 candidate locations calibrated by demographic persona (young professional, family, remote worker, student).

3. Commercial Business Viability

Feasibility scoring for 10+ retail categories (cafés, gyms, boutiques, dental clinics) calculating category saturation, complementary foot-traffic, and cluster strength.

4. 5-Year Trajectory Forecasting

Predictive urban lifecycle modeling matching a neighborhood's current archetype against historical analog progressions over time.

2. System Architecture

SmartRouter (Gemini)

Zero-shot entity resolution and intent parser turning arbitrary natural-language questions into structured API requests with graceful local regex fallbacks.

Spatial Signature Assembler

Normalizes Overpass 1km POI histograms, temperature seasonalities, and country-level GDP metrics into unit-scaled continuous vectors.

Fast Vector Retrieval

ChromaDB embedding index querying 98 hand-verified reference urban archetypes in <15ms.
