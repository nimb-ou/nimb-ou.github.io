The course

Both systems, derived rather than presented.

Thirty-one lessons. Each one states a problem, argues its way to an approach, writes the code into src/, runs it against real data, shows the output, and ends at a commit. Work through them in order and you will have built the systems, not read them.

≈45 hours end to end Exercises in every lesson Worked solutions ★ = the ones with negative results in them

Notebooks are for thinking

A lesson explores, plots, argues, and arrives at a decision. The decided-upon logic then goes into src/ as a tested function, and the lesson imports it back. No notebook is ever a dependency of the running service.

Sources are Python, not JSON

Lessons live as percent-format .py files; the .ipynb are generated with no stored outputs. Notebooks are painful in git — JSON diffs, embedded outputs, execution-count conflicts — and this sidesteps all of it. A test enforces that the two stay in sync.

The numbers regenerate

Every figure quoted in a lesson is read from a metrics.json produced by make all. If you re-run and get something different, the lesson is wrong and should be corrected — not the pipeline.

Exercises, and why several of them fail on purpose

Every lesson ends with exercises, and every exercise has a worked solution. Some solutions end in "there is no clean answer" — those are not cop-outs, they are the point.

An example that fails instructively

Lesson 01 asks you to price a single-rule policy: decline everyone whose checking account is overdrawn. It uses the strongest single predictor in the dataset.

It costs 0.912 per applicant against 0.700 for declining every application. A rule can be genuinely informative and still be a losing policy, because informative is about ranking and policy is about where you cut. Being told that is not the same as computing it.

An example with no good answer

Lesson 11 asks you to write the adverse-action notice you would send if age were the top-ranked factor and nothing else cleared the noise threshold.

Every option fails: citing age is not defensible, citing the next factor is false, an empty notice breaches the duty, and "multiple factors were considered" is the exact non-reason the regulation prohibits. You are meant to reach that dead end and conclude the fix is upstream.
