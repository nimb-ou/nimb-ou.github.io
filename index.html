Engineering Principles

Most of the engineering across each repository exists to make claims verifiable rather than merely impressive.

Measured, not remembered

Key performance metrics are read from automated build artifacts that regenerate from raw data on every push. Nothing is typed by hand.

Reported against hard baselines

Models are benchmarked against the strongest domain baselines, not easy strawmen. Improvements are tested across regime changes.

Negative results preserved

Hypotheses that failed in testing are preserved and documented in code comments and ablation tables rather than swept under the rug.
